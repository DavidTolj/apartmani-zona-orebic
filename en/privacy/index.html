Privacy and enquiry processing

Privacy contact

The host of Apartmani Zona, Josipa bana Jelačića 24, 20250 Orebić, Croatia, is responsible for processing enquiry and booking data. Contact anteozona@gmail.com with questions or requests.

Data and purposes

The form collects your name, email, stay dates, guest numbers and selected unit; telephone and a free-text message are optional. Required fields allow us to answer your specific enquiry. Do not use this form to send payment card details, identity document copies or sensitive data.

Legal bases

Replying to your request, preparing an offer and arranging accommodation are steps taken at your request before a contract; a confirmed stay is processed to perform that contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Statutory records and written complaints are processed to comply with legal obligations (point (c)). Necessary technical logs, abuse prevention and protection of legal claims rely on legitimate interests (point (f)). Sending an enquiry is not marketing consent.

Who receives data

The host receives enquiries at anteozona@gmail.com. Dalmatia Web Studio (CAFE BAR ZONA, sole proprietorship owned by Jana Tolj) receives a copy at dalmatiawebstudio@gmail.com to help the host handle the enquiry, not for the Studio’s own marketing. Netlify provides hosting and form processing; Google provides these email accounts. Data is shared with authorised authorities where a legal obligation applies.

Processing outside the EEA

Netlify and Google may process data outside the European Economic Area, including the United States. Their transfer information describes adequacy decisions where applicable and standard contractual clauses for other covered transfers: Netlify and Google. Ask the host about the safeguards applicable to your enquiry and how to obtain a copy.

Retention

Enquiry data is retained while responding and arranging the requested stay. Once communication and the need to arrange the stay end, data without another retention basis is deleted. Booking data and statutory records are retained for applicable legal periods; written complaint records for one year from receipt. Data necessary for a specific legal claim is retained until it is resolved and applicable time limits expire. These criteria also apply to support copies of enquiries.

Your rights

Subject to GDPR conditions, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction and data portability. You may object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw it without affecting earlier lawful processing. Send requests to the host; we normally respond within one month. You can complain to the Croatian Personal Data Protection Agency (AZOP).

Cookies and external links

This site uses no analytics or marketing cookies. Hosting may keep technical logs such as IP address, access time and requested page for security and operation. WhatsApp, Google, Facebook, Instagram and Apartmanija links open separate services with their own privacy policies. We do not make automated decisions or profile guests in ways producing legal or similarly significant effects.
