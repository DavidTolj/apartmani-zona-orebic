EXPERIENCE PELJEŠAC

Mornings beneath St. Ilija. Days by the sea. Evenings facing Korčula.

Orebić is more than a place to sleep. It is a seaside walk, swimming in crystal-clear coves, views towards Korčula and the aroma of fish on the grill.

Trstenica Beach

Orebić's main beach is only a few minutes' walk away.

Orebić promenade

Restaurants, cafés, shops and an evening walk along the waterfront.

Pelješac Channel panorama

A view from Viganj towards Korčula and one of the most beautiful parts of Pelješac.

Crystal-clear Adriatic

Quiet coves and clear water for long summer days.

Zona Grill

A shared space for relaxed evenings and a Dalmatian dinner.
